### Contribution Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines when submitting a pull request to this repository.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

✅ **Before You Submit**

-   Search existing issues and pull requests to avoid duplicates.
-   Only suggest tools, apps or methods that are truly awesome and have improved your productivity.
-   If your suggestion overlaps with, substitutes or strongly duplicates existing content, clearly justify in your pull request why this one should be added or kept instead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

📦 **Submitting a Contribution**

Contributions can either be to:

1.  Suggest new categories or improvements to existing categorization.

2.  Add new entries to the bottom of the most relevant category. In that case, use the following format:

```         
[Resource Name](link) – Short description.
```

Keep descriptions brief and clear, starting with a capital letter and ending with a full stop (period).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

📝 **Pull Request Requirements**

Use a clear and meaningful title: `Add [Resource Name] to [Category]` or `Add [New Category] for [Purpose]`

Include:

-   [] A link to the resource
-   [] A brief explanation of why it should be included

------------------------------------------------------------------------

📚 **Additional Guidance**

If you are unsure whether your contribution fits or how it should be styled, read the [Awesome Manifesto](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome/blob/master/awesome.md) .

------------------------------------------------------------------------
